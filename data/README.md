# Research data layout

Keep research recordings separate from application replay files.

- `raw/` original downloaded research datasets
- `processed/` derived tables after documented preprocessing
- `features/` model-ready features
- `synthetic/` clearly marked synthetic research experiments

SIH demonstration CSVs stay in `assets/demo/` and must remain labeled as simulation.

Do not invent fall, dehydration, disaster-survivor, or diagnostic labels from the current PhysioNet or Empatica research sets.

See [DATASETS.md](../DATASETS.md).
